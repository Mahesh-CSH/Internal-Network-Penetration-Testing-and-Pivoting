# IRC Enumeration

## Target

`10.10.10.130`

## Services

- TCP/6667
- TCP/6697

## Findings

UnrealIRCd 3.2.8.1 was identified.

The service was reviewed for known vulnerabilities during
controlled vulnerability validation.

## Security Considerations

- Upgrade or remove obsolete IRC software.
- Restrict access to trusted networks.
- Monitor unexpected IRC services.
