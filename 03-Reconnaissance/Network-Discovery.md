# Network Discovery

## Objective

Identify live hosts within the authorized laboratory environment and
determine the systems available for further assessment.

## Lab Targets

|       Host        |  IP Address  |      Role       |
|-------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Windows 10 Client | 10.10.10.128 | Pivot Host      |
| Metasploitable 2  | 10.10.10.130 | Internal Target |

## Approach

Network discovery and port scanning were performed from Kali Linux
against the authorized laboratory systems.

The assessment progressed from host identification to full TCP
service enumeration.

## Result

Both Windows 10 and Metasploitable 2 were identified as active
laboratory systems and were subsequently assessed within the defined
scope.
