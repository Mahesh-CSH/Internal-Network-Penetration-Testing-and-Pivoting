## Objective

Establish controlled access to the Windows 10 pivot host.

## Session

A Meterpreter reverse TCP session was successfully established from
the Windows 10 laboratory system to Kali Linux.

		Kali:    192.168.88.1xx
		Windows: 192.168.88.136
